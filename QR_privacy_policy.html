Privacy Policy for NexZ QR

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

At NexZ QR, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

NexZ QR is designed with privacy in mind. We minimize data collection to ensure a safe user experience.

  • Camera Access: Required solely to scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. We do not store, record, or transmit camera footage or images to any server.
  • Storage Access / Photo Library: Required if you choose to save generated/scanned QR codes as images to your device or print them.
  • Scanned & Generated Data: All QR code generation, scanning, printing, and sharing features operate locally on your device. Your scanned history and generated codes remain on your device unless you choose to share them manually.

2. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our app may integrate third-party services (such as Google AdMob) to serve advertisements. These services may collect certain non-personally identifiable information, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA / AAID)
  • IP address (for coarse location/country level ad delivery)

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Security

Since the core features (scanning, generating, printing, sharing) function locally on your device, your scan data is not stored on external servers, minimizing privacy risks.

4. Children's Privacy

NexZ QR does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
